# Omarchy Nigeria: a green, white and green desktop for Independence Day

For Independence Day, this project brings a little more Nigeria to the everyday desktop: green surfaces, white lettering, and photographs of our cities.

The theme pairs a soft green background with bright emerald accents. Off-white text, tuned for long reading sessions, keeps the workspace clear, while supporting colours make terminal messages easy to tell apart. The focused window wears an emerald, white and emerald border, like the flag.

The wallpapers are twenty high-resolution photographs: the National Theatre and Carter Bridge in Lagos, the National Mosque and the City Gate in Abuja, Zuma Rock at dusk, the green ridges of the Obudu Plateau, the Emir's Palace in Dutse, Gidan Makama in Kano, the creeks of Port Harcourt, and more. They change every fifteen minutes, in no particular order, so the desktop keeps surprising you. Every photograph is credited to the person who took it: their name sits in the corner of the desktop and the lock screen, and one click takes you to the rest of their work.

The theme is built for Omarchy, using a small palette file that its templates turn into application settings. The aim is something simple enough to install, adapt, and share.
